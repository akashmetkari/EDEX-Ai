# 💡 EDEX-Ai: Empowering Safe & Informed Choices through RAG-based AI

### 🚀 Revolutionizing Sex Education for the Modern Age using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

---

## ❓ Problem Identified

Despite being a fundamental part of human life, **Sex Education remains neglected, tabooed, and inconsistent**, especially in conservative or developing societies. Millions of young adults and even grown-ups lack access to **trustworthy, age-appropriate, and scientifically accurate information** about sex, consent, contraception, STDs, relationships, and safe practices.

This lack of knowledge often leads to:

- Unsafe sex practices  
- Rising STD rates  
- Teenage pregnancies  
- Mental trauma due to misinformation  
- Poor understanding of consent and emotional boundaries  

---

## 💡 Our Solution: EDEX-Ai

**EDEX-Ai** is an **AI-powered, RAG-based conversational assistant** built to democratize access to verified, non-judgmental sex education using state-of-the-art language models.

It retrieves accurate responses from **two highly credible books**:

1. 🧠 *The New Naked: The Ultimate Sex Education for Grown-ups*  
2. 📚 *The Complete Book of Sex Education (PDFDrive)*  

Using **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)**, the system combines the power of **Large Language Models (LLMs)** with a curated document store to deliver context-aware, medically accurate answers to any sex-ed question, in real time.

---

## 🧠 How AI Is Integrated

- ✅ **RAG Architecture**: Combines semantic search (via embeddings) + LLM for factual correctness  
- ✅ **Book-Based QA**: Context is extracted *only* from the two books to ensure scientific grounding  
- ✅ **User Query Understanding**: Handles sensitive, awkward, or vague questions empathetically  
- ✅ **Privacy-Focused Interface**: Encourages learning without judgment or shame
